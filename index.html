LEVEL 1 · THE BOSS

Academic researchers all know the struggle…

Record-keeping is a chore which is squeezed around complex experiments.

Why it happens

Researchers in academic biochemistry labs struggle to record their activities due to contamination risks, the complexity and time sensitivity of experiments, and the length of time required to be in the lab.

  • Information is lost
  • Burden falls on the researcher
  • Results are not reproducible
$28bn lost per year to irreproducible life-sciences research
>50% of life-sciences research is irreproducible
36% of that cost is lost materials
26% is data analysis & reporting

Source: Freedman, L. P. et al. (2015), PLOS Biology. A single experiment can run for over a week.
